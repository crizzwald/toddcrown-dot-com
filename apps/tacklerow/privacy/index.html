Tacklerow

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 22, 2026

The short version

Tacklerow is a marketplace, so it needs an account and keeps what's required to run orders, messages, and payouts. It doesn't sell your personal information or build advertising profiles. The full policy is at tacklerow.com/privacy.

What's collected

Your email, password, and basic profile details such as a display name. If you sell, the shop and listings you publish. Orders you buy or sell, including the shipping address. Messages you send to other buyers and sellers. Standard log and device information used to keep the service working and secure.

Payments

Card details go straight to Stripe and are never seen or stored by Tacklerow. Only a record that an order was paid and a reference token from Stripe are kept. Seller payouts are also handled by Stripe.

Who processes your data

Supabase (database, sign-in, and photo storage), Stripe (payments and payouts), Resend (order and account email), and Vercel (website hosting). Each handles only the part of the job it needs.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your information is kept while your account is open. You can view and edit most of it in the app, and you can email support@tacklerow.com for a copy or to delete your account. Order and payment records are kept longer because tax, refund, and dispute rules require it.

Children

Tacklerow is for adults. You must be at least 18 to use it, and it doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach me at support@tacklerow.com.
