Tacklerow

Handmade fishing lures from US makers

A US marketplace for handmade fishing tackle. Every bait is hand-poured, painted, tied, or built by a real maker, and you buy it straight from them.

View on the App Store

Features

  • Baits for your fish Every listing is tagged by species. Browse and search soft plastics, crankbaits, jigs, spinnerbaits, and flies.
  • Meet the maker Real photos of every bait, the person who made it, and their full shop. Message a maker about colors, sizes, or a custom order.
  • Buy with confidence Secure checkout through Stripe, with tracking from paid to shipped to delivered. Fill one cart from several makers.
  • Sell your baits Open a storefront, list your baits, ship with tracking, and get paid to your bank through secure payouts.
