GetUp Jump Higher

Plyometrics for a higher vertical

Jump higher in 13 weeks. The GetUp plyometric program builds your endurance first, then your power, with every workout planned for you and a video for every exercise.

View on the App Store

Features

  • Build the base, then the power An 8-week Endurance phase, then a 5-week Power phase.
  • Every workout, on schedule Two workouts a week, every third day, dated for you and added to your calendar if you like.
  • Check off every rep Sets, reps and rest for each exercise, plus added weight and your vertical.
  • Learn the right form A video for all seven exercises, with the coaching cue for each.
  • See how high you've gone Every phase you finish, with your best vertical jump.
  • Unlock the full program The first three workouts of each phase are free. A one-time purchase unlocks the rest, every exercise video, and the calendar.
