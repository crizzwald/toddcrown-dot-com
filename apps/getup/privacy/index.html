GetUp Jump Higher

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 20, 2026

The short version

GetUp Jump Higher doesn't collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, analytics, ads, or tracking.

Calendar

If you choose to add workouts to your calendar, the app asks for calendar access and only creates those workout events. It does not read or upload your calendar.

Data stored on your device

Your workout progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app removes it.

Children

The app doesn't knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
