GetUp Jump Higher

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 23, 2026

The short version

GetUp Jump Higher doesn't have accounts, and it contains no analytics, ads, or tracking. Nothing about you is collected or stored off your device.

Exercise videos

The form videos are hosted by Apple with the app and downloaded from Apple's servers the first time you play each one. Nothing about you is sent with that download, and Apple handles it under its own privacy policy.

Calendar

If you choose to add workouts to your calendar, the app asks for calendar access and creates its own GetUp Jump Higher calendar with your workout dates. It doesn't read or upload your other events, and you can delete that calendar from the app's settings at any time.

Purchases

The unlock is handled entirely by Apple. We never receive your payment information.

Data stored on your device

Your phases, workout progress, weights, verticals, and notes are saved on your device, along with any videos you've downloaded. Deleting the app removes them.

Children

The app doesn't knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
