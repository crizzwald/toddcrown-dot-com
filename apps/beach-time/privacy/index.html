Beach Time

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 20, 2026

The short version

Beach Time doesn't have accounts, and it contains no analytics, ads, or tracking.

Location

If you allow it, your approximate location is used to find nearby tide stations. The coordinates are sent to the tide data service only to look up stations and are not tied to you or stored by the app. You can turn location off at any time in Settings.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never receive your payment information.

Data stored on your device

Your favorite stations are saved on your device. Deleting the app removes them.

Children

The app doesn't knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
