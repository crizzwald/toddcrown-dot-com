Beach Time

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 23, 2026

The short version

Beach Time doesn't have accounts, and it contains no analytics, ads, or tracking. Nothing about you is collected or stored off your device.

Tide data

Tide predictions are downloaded directly from NOAA's public tide service. The app only asks for the tide station you're viewing; nothing about you is sent. Like any website, NOAA's servers can see your IP address.

Location

If you allow it, your location is used to find the tide stations closest to you. The list of stations is built into the app, so that match happens entirely on your device and your location is never sent anywhere. You can turn location off at any time in Settings.

Search and maps

When you search by city or ZIP code, that search text is sent to Apple's MapKit service to find the place, and station maps are shown with Apple Maps. Both are covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Tide reports

Morning tide reports are local notifications scheduled on your device. The app refreshes tide data in the background so each report is current. No push notification service or server is involved, and no device token is collected.

Purchases

The Favorites unlock is handled entirely by Apple. We never receive your payment information.

Data stored on your device

Your favorite beaches, tide report settings, and a cache of recent tide data are saved on your device. Deleting the app removes them.

Children

The app doesn't knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
