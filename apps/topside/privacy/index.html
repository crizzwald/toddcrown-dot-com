Topside

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 20, 2026

The short version

Topside doesn't collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking.

Map condition data

To show the schedule, the app downloads the publicly available map conditions page from arcraiders.com. That request is made directly from your device, the same as visiting the website in a browser. Like any website, that server can see your IP address; Topside sends nothing else.

Notifications

Alerts are local notifications scheduled on your device. No push notification service or server is involved, and no device token is collected.

Data stored on your device

Your region, alert settings, and watched conditions and maps are saved on your device only. A copy of the latest schedule is cached so the app and widgets load quickly. Deleting the app removes all of it.

Children

The app doesn't knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
