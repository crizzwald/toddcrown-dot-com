Infinite Spartan Stats

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 27, 2026

The short version

Infinite Spartan Stats has no servers of its own, and it contains no analytics, ads, or tracking. Nothing about you is collected or stored off your device.

Signing in with Xbox

You sign in through Microsoft's own sign-in page. The app never sees your password. Microsoft returns sign-in tokens, which are stored in your device's keychain and used only to request Halo Infinite and Xbox data from Microsoft, Xbox Live, and Halo Waypoint services.

Your use of those services is covered by the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Stats and Xbox friends

Stats, matches, ranks, and loadouts are requested directly from Halo Waypoint. Nothing goes through any other server.

When you search, the app asks Xbox Live for matching gamertags and for your Xbox friends list, so you can pick a friend to look up. The friends list is only shown in the app; it isn't saved or sent anywhere else. Gamertags and Halo stats are public game information.

Data stored on your device

Your sign-in, the Spartans you've recently looked up, and saved matches, maps, and pictures are kept on your device so the app opens quickly. Clear Saved Data in the app's settings removes the saved matches and pictures, signing out removes your sign-in, and deleting the app removes everything.

Children

The app doesn't knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
