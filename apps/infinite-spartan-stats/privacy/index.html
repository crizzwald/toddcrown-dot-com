Infinite Spartan Stats

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 20, 2026

The short version

Infinite Spartan Stats has no servers of its own that store your data, and it contains no analytics, ads, or tracking.

Signing in with Xbox

You sign in through Microsoft's own sign-in page. The app never sees your password. Microsoft returns access tokens, which are stored on your device and used only to request your Halo Infinite data from Microsoft, Xbox Live, and Halo Waypoint services.

Your use of those services is covered by the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Stats and clips

Stats are requested directly from Halo Waypoint services. To show game clips, the gamertag you're viewing is sent to a small proxy that retrieves them from a third-party Halo stats service (HaloDotAPI). Gamertags and stats are public game information.

Data stored on your device

Sign-in tokens and your recent searches are stored on your device. Signing out, turning on the reset option in the app's section of the iOS Settings app, or deleting the app removes them.

Children

The app doesn't knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
